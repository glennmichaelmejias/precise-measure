Which iPhones does it work on?

The camera tools need an iPhone with a LiDAR scanner: iPhone 12 Pro, 12 Pro Max or a later Pro model, running iOS 17 or later.

Tips for accurate measurements

  • Move the iPhone slowly at first so it can map the surroundings.
  • Good, even light helps. Turn on the flashlight (top left) in dim places.
  • For woodworking accuracy, hold the phone at arm's length or closer.
  • Wait for the badge at the top to turn orange: the pointer has locked onto a point, corner or edge.

The tools

  • Measure: distances between points, snapping to corners and edges.
  • Ruler: a true-size ruler on the screen for small parts. Lay the part on the screen and read it in mm, cm or inches.
  • Cut (Woodworking): finds where a piece you need fits on a scrap board.
  • Height (Site): heights of trees, walls and buildings.
  • Area: surfaces in any orientation, or land area in Site mode.
  • Level: a water level. Set a reference height, then see how far above or below it any spot is.
