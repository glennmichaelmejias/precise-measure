Privacy Policy

Precise Measure (shown as Precise on the home screen) · Last updated November 25, 2026

The short version

Precise Measure does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any information about you. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it makes no network connections.

Camera and LiDAR

The app uses the iPhone camera and LiDAR scanner to measure what you point it at. The camera picture and depth data are processed on your iPhone while you measure and are not saved or sent anywhere.

Data kept on your iPhone

The app remembers your preferences (such as the units and which side the screen ruler is on) on your iPhone. This stays on the device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
